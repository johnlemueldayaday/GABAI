## Ready-to-Use Safer Revisions

### Background of the Study

Current claim:

> According to the Philippine Statistics Authority (2023), ... low- to middle-income families spending as much as 42.7 percent of their monthly earnings on food and non-alcoholic beverages alone.

Suggested revision:

> Data from the Philippine Statistics Authority show that food remains one of the largest components of Filipino family expenditure, particularly for lower-income households. This supports the need for tools that help households monitor grocery spending and plan purchases more deliberately.

Current claim:

> DICT (2022) reported that smartphone penetration in the Philippines has exceeded 73 percent of the total population.

Suggested revision:

> Reports on the Philippine digital landscape indicate that internet and mobile connectivity are now widely used by Filipinos, making mobile applications a practical channel for household financial management tools.

### AI-Powered Budgeting and Financial Management Tools

Current claim:

> Zhao et al. (2023) demonstrated that LLM-based financial assistants are capable of generating contextually accurate budget recommendations...

Suggested revision:

> Recent research on large language models suggests that they can support natural-language interaction and domain-specific reasoning tasks, but their use in personal finance should be carefully constrained, validated, and supplemented with transparent user controls.

Use Brown et al. (2020) for general LLM capability and Wu et al. (2023) for finance-domain LLM evidence. Do not use the current Zhao et al. reference.

Current claim:

> Wu et al. (2023) developed FinMA...

Suggested revision:

> Wu et al. (2023) introduced BloombergGPT, a large language model trained on financial and general-domain data, showing the value of domain-specific training for finance-related language tasks.

### Mobile Grocery Management Applications

Current claim:

> Alharthi et al. (2019) ...

Suggested revision:

> Mobile grocery tools commonly support list creation, categorization, price tracking, and purchase planning, but the manuscript should cite a source specifically about grocery applications or mobile shopping behavior.

Do not cite Alharthi et al. here because the paper is about cloud migration.

### Household Budget Management in the Philippine Context

Current claim:

> BSP (2021) ... Only 29 percent of surveyed Filipino adults reported using any form of structured budgeting tool...

Suggested revision:

> The BSP Consumer Finance Survey shows that food and beverages consumed at home accounted for the largest expenditure share among Filipino households in 2021, while housing and utilities were also major household expenses. These findings support the relevance of grocery and bill-management features in GABAY.

### AI Chatbot Assistants

Current claim:

> Nicoletti (2021) documented that AI-powered financial chatbots reduced customer service response times by an average of 60 percent...

Suggested revision:

> Nicoletti (2017) discusses the broader integration of financial services and technology. For claims about chatbot performance, the manuscript should use a more direct empirical chatbot study.

### Expense Tracking and Analytics

Current claim:

> Borgo et al. (2022) ... chart-based expense reporting...

Suggested revision:

> Prior research on visualization and decision-making indicates that visual representations can improve comprehension when users need to interpret complex information. In GABAY, charts are therefore used to help users review spending patterns more quickly than text-only summaries.

Use a verified visualization source instead of the current Borgo et al. citation if the exact article cannot be provided.

### Bill Tracking and Payment Management

Current claim:

> Fang and Zhao (2020) ... households using digital bill reminder applications reduced late payments by 58 percent...

Suggested revision:

> Digital reminders can help users keep due dates visible and reduce reliance on memory when managing recurring bills. Because the exact Fang and Zhao source could not be verified, the manuscript should either provide the original source or revise the statement as a design rationale rather than a quantified empirical finding.
