# Gabay Panel Presentation and Live Demo Script

A clean, step by step walkthrough designed to showcase the full vision, technical depth, and real world value of Gabay to your evaluation panel.


# Presentation Overview

Target Duration: 7 to 10 minutes
Presenter Mindset: Confident, structured, narrative driven
Core Theme: Transforming everyday budgeting from a chore into an effortless, intelligent daily guide for Filipinos.


# Part 1: The Hook and Problem Statement

Duration: 1 minute

What to say:
Good morning esteemed members of the panel. Most personal finance apps fail because they demand too much manual bookkeeping, or they do not reflect the way Filipinos actually spend. Many of us juggle physical cash, GCash, Maya, and bank accounts, while constantly worrying whether we can safely spend money before the next payday. Grocery shopping is another blind spot, where fluctuating prices between supermarkets like SM and Puregold quietly drain household budgets.

What to show:
Hold up your phone or show the screen displaying Gabay installed as a Progressive Web App on your home screen. Emphasize that it opens in full screen with zero browser clutter, just like a native application.


# Part 2: First Impressions on the Dashboard

Duration: 1.5 minutes

Step 1. Launch the app from the home screen
Highlight the quick load time, smooth animations, and the welcoming 3D avatar that gives immediate, friendly feedback about your financial status.

Step 2. Explain the Total Balance card
Show how the top section aggregates all user funds across physical wallets and digital accounts into one clear number.

Step 3. Showcase the Safe to Spend algorithm
Point directly to the Safe to Spend card.
Explain that unlike traditional budgeting apps that only show historical totals, Gabay looks forward. It calculates how much money the user can safely spend today based on their next payday, remaining days in the pay cycle, and upcoming obligations.
Show the daily allocation indicator and how the progress bar dynamically reacts to expenditures.


# Part 3: Multi Wallet Management and Transfers

Duration: 1.5 minutes

Step 1. Navigate to the Wallets section
Show your configured accounts such as Cash, GCash, BPI, and Landbank.
Point out that each account shows its live balance clearly.

Step 2. Perform a live transfer
Tap Transfer Money.
Select Cash as the source account.
Select GCash as the destination account.
Enter an amount, such as 500 pesos.
Confirm the transfer.

What to tell the panel:
Notice how the transfer automatically updates both balances simultaneously without creating fake expense entries. It keeps financial tracking true to reality.


# Part 4: Smart Grocery Cart and Price Intelligence

Duration: 2.5 minutes

Step 1. Open the Grocery Cart page
Explain that groceries represent one of the biggest recurring expenses for Filipino families.

Step 2. Select a store
Choose SM Supermarket or Puregold from the store selector.
Explain that Gabay includes built in price databases customized to specific supermarket chains. When you change stores, prices automatically reflect the selected merchant.

Step 3. Build a quick cart
Add a few staples such as chicken, soy sauce, and cooking oil.
Show the live running total and budget comparison bar at the top of the cart.
Show that items can be checked off in real time as you walk down supermarket aisles.

Step 4. Complete checkout
Tap the finish trip button.
Select the account used for payment, such as GCash.
Confirm the transaction.

What to tell the panel:
In one tap, the grocery trip moves into trip history, records price data for future intelligence, and automatically creates an expense record in your financial ledger without double counting the total.


# Part 5: Bills Tracker and Calendar Sync

Duration: 1.5 minutes

Step 1. Open the Bills page
Show the clean calendar view with highlighted due dates.

Step 2. Add an upcoming bill
Create an event such as Electric Bill or Internet Subscription.
Set the due date, amount, and payment frequency.
Save the bill.

What to tell the panel:
Gabay keeps track of every recurring obligation and factors these upcoming bills directly into your Safe to Spend budget. Users are never surprised by an empty wallet when bill day arrives.


# Part 6: Technical Architecture and Deployment

Duration: 1 minute

What to explain to the panel:

1. Flutter Framework
Built with Flutter for high performance, smooth animations, and reactive state management across all screens.

2. Cross Platform and PWA Architecture
Instead of requiring expensive store certificates and facing 7 day developer build expirations, Gabay is deployed as an enterprise grade Progressive Web App using Firebase Hosting.

3. Offline Persistence
All core calculations, wallets, transactions, and grocery databases are persisted locally on the device, meaning the app remains functional even inside supermarket basements with spotty mobile data.


# Part 7: Closing and Call for Questions

Duration: 30 seconds

What to say:
Gabay bridges the gap between everyday groceries, multi account budgeting, and forward looking financial peace of mind. Thank you very much, and I am now ready for your questions and feedback.


# Anticipated Panel Questions and Ideal Answers

Question 1: What makes Gabay different from existing expense trackers like Money Lover or Wallet?
Answer: Most trackers are backward looking spreadsheets that only record what you already spent. Gabay provides a forward looking Safe to Spend engine tailored to Filipino payday rhythms, combined with store specific grocery pricing intelligence and multi wallet transfers.

Question 2: How does the app ensure data privacy?
Answer: Personal financial records and transaction histories are stored on device, giving the user complete ownership of their data without selling personal habits to third party advertisers.

Question 3: Why choose a Progressive Web App over native app store distribution?
Answer: PWA allows instant zero friction distribution. Users do not need to download heavy packages from app stores, updates deploy instantly through Firebase Hosting, and the app runs smoothly across Android, iOS, and desktop browsers while retaining a full screen native app experience.
